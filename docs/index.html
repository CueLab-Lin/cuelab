01 · CUE

整個資料夾丟進去，Cue 自己打好Drop in a folder. The cues set themselves.

一首歌大約 10 秒。調性、BPM、能量、人聲比例一起算好，寫進 Serato 前會先備份，你手動打過的歌自動跳過。About 10 seconds a track. Key, BPM, energy and vocals come with it. Your Serato cues are backed up before writing, and tracks you cued by hand are skipped.

下一首接什麼？先聽再說What's next? Hear it first.

照調性、速度、能量、人聲推薦下一首。按一下試聽：B 提早 4 小節不帶低音進來，到 A 的 F 那拍推過去、換低音，B 剛好落在 C。兩台精準對拍。Suggestions by key, tempo, energy and vocals. Press preview: B comes in 4 bars early without its bass, on A's F the fader goes over and the bass swaps, and B lands right on C. Both decks beat-matched.

03 · SET

一鍵排一整套 SetBuild a whole set in one click

選長度和能量走勢：暖場、一路往上、經典弧線、高峰時段。CueLab 從你的歌裡挑出接得順的順序。Pick a length and an energy shape: warm-up, always up, classic arc or peak time. CueLab picks an order from your tracks that mixes smoothly.

下載與安裝Download and install

1

下載、解壓縮Download and unzip

雙擊 CueLab 壓縮檔，把 CueLab.app 拖進「應用程式」。Double-click the zip and drag CueLab.app into Applications.

2

第一次：右鍵 → 打開First time: right-click → Open

3

加入歌曲，開始分析Add tracks and analyse

寫入 Serato 前記得先把 Serato DJ Pro 關掉。Quit Serato DJ Pro before writing your cues.
